# Can Permira create durable value in Squarespace after the take-private?

## Working Thesis
Permira completed the acquisition of Squarespace on October 17, 2024, and the transaction valued the company at approximately $7.2 billion, so the diligence question is now a sponsor-owned value-creation question rather than a public-shareholder voting question.

## Traceable Claims
- Permira completed the acquisition of Squarespace on October 17, 2024, and the transaction valued the company at approximately $7.2 billion, so the diligence question is now a sponsor-owned value-creation question rather than a public-shareholder voting question. (SRC-001)
- Squarespace's Q2 2024 total revenue was $296.8 million, up 20% year over year, which indicates the company entered the take-private with real top-line momentum rather than a stalled revenue base. (SRC-002)
- Annual run-rate revenue reached $1.179 billion in Q2 2024, up 20% year over year, while unique subscriptions increased 21% to more than 5.2 million, giving the sponsor a large subscription base to monetize. (SRC-002)
- Average revenue per unique subscription rose only 3% year over year to $225.45, which suggests that near-term value creation probably needs price-packaging, attachment, or mix improvements in addition to continued subscription growth. (SRC-002)
- Squarespace's own metric definition says ARRR is a run-rate indicator and not a GAAP revenue forecast, so underwriting should avoid treating ARRR as guaranteed next-year revenue. (SRC-002)
- Wix reported Q2 2024 bookings of $458.4 million, up 15% year over year, and attributed momentum partly to Wix Studio, AI capabilities, and commerce expansion, creating a competitive benchmark that Squarespace must answer with product depth and partner-led growth rather than brand alone. (SRC-003)
- The merger announcement warned of business disruption, employee-retention, financing, regulatory, and competitive-response risks, making continuity and execution quality explicit diligence gates after close. (SRC-004)
- Squarespace generated $72.1 million of adjusted EBITDA in Q2 2024 against $296.8 million of revenue, but adjusted EBITDA declined slightly year over year, so growth was not translating into automatic EBITDA expansion at the latest public checkpoint. (SRC-005)
- Unlevered free cash flow was $65.4 million in Q2 2024 and $154.7 million for the first half of 2024, giving the sponsor a cash-generative asset base for deleveraging, reinvestment, or tuck-in work. (SRC-005)
- Net cash provided by operating activities was $145.8 million for the first half of 2024 versus $116.7 million in the prior-year period, supporting the view that the company had improving cash conversion before delisting. (SRC-005)
