# Decomposition Axis Library

Use axes as priors and reasoning tools, not canned templates. Always justify why the chosen axis fits the problem frame.

| Axis | Best For | Core Question | Common Trap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Component/factor | Unfamiliar problem spaces | What are the parts of the system? | Freezing a first-cut map as the final tree |
| Causal driver | Diagnostics | What causes R1? | Mixing causes and fixes |
| Lever/intervention | Action planning | What can we change to reach R2? | Listing actions before proving causes |
| Supply/demand | Capacity, markets, resources | Is the gap from supply, demand, or matching? | Ignoring constraints and substitutability |
| Incidence/severity | Health, safety, quality, reliability | Is the issue frequency, consequence, or both? | Treating severity as a footnote |
| Process/value chain | Operations, service delivery, implementation | Where in the flow does performance break? | Flattening sequence into topics |
| Funnel/conversion | Sales, adoption, usage, recruiting | Where do entities drop off? | Ignoring quality or economics of conversion |
| Financial equation | Profit, ROI, valuation, unit economics | Which mathematical driver moves the result? | Hiding behavioral assumptions under numbers |
| Criteria/option | Choice among alternatives | Which option best satisfies R2? | Treating all criteria as equal hard gates |
| Stakeholder/incentive | Policy, org change, ecosystems | Who must act, and why would they? | Stakeholder list with no mechanism |
| Constraint gate | Go/no-go decisions | What can kill the recommendation? | Overusing gates when trade-offs are possible |
| Uncertainty strategy | Long horizons, ambiguous futures | What should we do under uncertainty? | Single base case masquerading as strategy |
| Portfolio/options | Innovation, investments, philanthropic strategy | Which set of moves creates robust expected value? | Choosing one bet too early |
| System feedback | Wicked problems, platforms, networks | How do actions change the system? | Linear causal tree for a feedback problem |
| Evidence sufficiency | Scientific/legal/regulatory questions | What evidence would be enough to decide? | Counting sources instead of evaluating quality |
| Comparative benchmark | Performance gaps | Compared to what, and why is the gap present? | Benchmarking without causal explanation |

## Axis Selection Checks

1. Does this axis answer the governing question?
2. Does it expose a mechanism, decision criterion, or controllable lever?
3. Are sibling branches naturally non-overlapping?
4. Does it lead to evidence the team can gather?
5. What important branch would this axis hide?
6. Does a quick calculation or example show that the cut will produce insight?
