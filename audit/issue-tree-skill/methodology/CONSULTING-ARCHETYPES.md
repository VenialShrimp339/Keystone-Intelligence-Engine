# Consulting Archetypes

These are examples and priors, not templates to apply blindly.

## Market Sizing

Useful axes:

- demand build-up: population/accounts x eligibility x adoption x usage x price
- supply/constraint: capacity, channel reach, regulation, production, distribution
- triangulation: top-down, bottom-up, analogous market, competitor revenue, unit economics

Senior-quality move: state the required accuracy and use multiple bounding paths instead of false precision.

## Market Entry

Useful axes:

- market attractiveness
- right to win
- economics and investment case
- execution feasibility
- risks and constraints
- strategic fit and exit/options

Logic note: branches may be weighted factors or constraint gates. The skill must label which.

## Profitability / Performance Diagnostic

Useful axes:

- revenue: price, volume, mix, retention, market share
- cost: variable, fixed, overhead, productivity, input prices
- assets/capital: utilization, working capital, invested capital
- external factors: market, regulation, competitor response

Senior-quality move: use the financial equation, then place behavioral or operational causes beneath the affected driver.

## Pricing

Useful axes:

- customer willingness to pay
- demand elasticity and volume risk
- competitive response
- cost/margin impact
- channel and contract constraints
- implementation and communication

Senior-quality move: distinguish price increase from value capture mechanism. Volume loss may be acceptable if contribution improves.

## M&A / Diligence

Useful axes:

- investment thesis and value creation
- market attractiveness
- target quality
- standalone economics
- synergies or operational improvement
- risks, liabilities, and deal feasibility
- exit and downside protection

Senior-quality move: include what must be true for the deal to be worth doing and what would kill it early.

## Operations Diagnostic

Useful axes:

- demand/arrival load
- process flow and bottlenecks
- capacity and staffing mix
- quality/rework
- handoffs and downstream constraints
- measurement artifacts

Senior-quality move: separate true demand growth from throughput or measurement problems.

## Technical Architecture Decision

Useful axes:

- current failure diagnosis
- requirements and constraints
- architecture options
- trade-offs: reliability, velocity, security, cost, maintainability
- migration feasibility
- team capability and operating model

Senior-quality move: diagnose whether architecture is the cause before recommending a rebuild.

## Policy Decision

Useful axes:

- objective and target population
- mechanism of change
- distributional effects
- fiscal and administrative feasibility
- legal/regulatory constraints
- political feasibility
- externalities and unintended consequences

Senior-quality move: show trade-offs among objectives and identify least-bad options when no branch dominates.

## Scientific Funding / Evidence Decision

Useful axes:

- prior evidence strength
- biological or causal plausibility
- study quality and bias
- feasibility of decisive research
- expected value of information
- opportunity cost versus alternatives

Senior-quality move: separate "is the claim likely true?" from "is a new study worth funding?"
