# Anti-Patterns

## Data-First Fact Gathering

Symptoms: broad research begins before the tree states what would prove or disprove the answer.

Correction: formulate provisional hypotheses and issue-tree questions before data collection.

## Hypothesis Attachment

Symptoms: the model treats the initial hypothesis as the answer and seeks confirming evidence.

Correction: list must-be-true assumptions and easiest disproofs; invite revision.

## Uniform Completeness

Symptoms: every branch is investigated to equal depth.

Correction: use key drivers, dominant questions, and quick wins to sequence depth.

## Boiling The Ocean

Symptoms: many analyses are proposed because they are interesting.

Correction: require each analysis to prove/disprove a branch or change a decision.

## False Precision

Symptoms: estimates appear precise despite uncertain inputs and no decision need for that accuracy.

Correction: state required accuracy and use directional ranges where sufficient.

## Missing End Product

Symptoms: leaves describe research topics without saying what artifact resolves them.

Correction: attach expected end product to every leaf.

## No So-What

Symptoms: branches or evidence requirements do not state decision implication.

Correction: require every leaf to answer "what would this tell us and why would it matter?"

## Framework Defensiveness

Symptoms: the output presents a consulting framework in a way that feels generic or imported.

Correction: use framework concepts to generate problem-specific questions; avoid naming frameworks unless useful.
