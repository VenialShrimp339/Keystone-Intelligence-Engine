# Anti-Patterns

## Topic Inventory Disguised as an Issue Tree

Symptoms:

- Top branches are broad topics like market, operations, finance, people, technology.
- The governing question is absent or generic.
- Branches could appear under many unrelated prompts.

Correction:

- Reconstruct the reader question.
- Restate the tree root as a decision or diagnostic question.
- Rebuild branches as causes, reasons, criteria, steps, or options that answer that question.

## Mixed Logic at the Same Level

Symptoms:

- A sibling group mixes causes, recommendations, risks, and metrics.
- One branch is an action, another is a reason, another is a stakeholder.
- The parent cannot be summarized cleanly.

Correction:

- Choose one logic mode for the group.
- Move off-axis items down a level or into evidence, constraints, risks, or implementation.

## Empty Parent Labels

Symptoms:

- Parent labels say "key issues," "main drivers," "considerations," or "workstreams."
- The label does not state what the children imply.
- A reader must infer the point.

Correction:

- Ask why these children and no others are grouped.
- State the insight, effect, or decision implication above them.

## MECE Theater

Symptoms:

- The answer says branches are MECE but gives no principle of division.
- Branches overlap or omit obvious parts.
- The same branch could be classified under multiple siblings.

Correction:

- Declare the grouping source: sequence, structure, or class.
- Define the universe being divided.
- Test for overlaps and omissions against that universe.

## Data-First Consulting

Symptoms:

- The plan begins with interviews, surveys, benchmarking, or data gathering before a diagnostic model.
- Evidence requests are broad and not tied to hypotheses.
- The answer cannot say when research is finished.

Correction:

- Build a diagnostic framework from the opening-scene structure or process.
- Generate yes/no questions.
- Gather only evidence needed to confirm or exclude branches.

## Premature Solution Tree

Symptoms:

- Recommendations appear before causes are diagnosed.
- The tree is organized around interventions without proving they address R1.
- The same issue appears as both cause and fix.

Correction:

- Separate diagnostic tree from solution tree.
- Require evidence that the diagnosed cause is material before retaining the fix.

## Open-Ended "Issues"

Symptoms:

- Branches are phrased as "understand X," "evaluate Y," or "what is Z?"
- No branch has a yes/no resolution criterion.
- Research cannot close.

Correction:

- Convert concerns into yes/no issues.
- Attach evidence that would answer each issue.
- Prioritize issues by ability to change the decision.

## Alternative Straw-Manning

Symptoms:

- The tree presents several options mainly to eliminate them.
- The recommended option is justified by competitors' weaknesses rather than by decision criteria.
- Alternatives were not live in the reader's mind.

Correction:

- If alternatives are known, evaluate them against criteria derived from R2.
- If alternatives are generated, first prove the option universe is complete.
- Justify the recommendation by fit to the problem.

## Over-Abstract Inference

Symptoms:

- The parent claim says more than the children prove.
- Evidence indicators are turned into broad strategic conclusions.
- The tree feels polished but untestable.

Correction:

- Lower the parent abstraction until it is specifically supported.
- Add missing children if the broader claim is needed.
- State confidence and unresolved tests.

## Cause-Effect Compression

Symptoms:

- A sequence is flattened into a list.
- Intermediate causes and effects are siblings.
- Actions are mixed with the results they produce.

Correction:

- Visualize the process.
- Separate steps, intermediate outputs, and final effects.
- Place lower-level activities beneath the end product they create.

## Framework Substitution

Symptoms:

- The answer applies a familiar framework because it is recognizable.
- The framework does not map to the opening scene or R1/R2 gap.
- Important prompt-specific facts disappear.

Correction:

- Start from the problem's structure or process.
- Use standard frameworks only when they match the causal system.
- Preserve prompt-specific constraints as branch tests or decision criteria.

## No Pruning Rationale

Symptoms:

- The full tree and pruned tree are identical.
- Branches are prioritized by generic importance.
- Deferred branches are not explained.

Correction:

- Prune by decision impact, likelihood, evidence cost, and discriminating power.
- State why retained branches can change the answer.
- State why deferred branches are lower-value now.
