## What "MVP" Means Here

An honest MVP for this project is:

- the core analytical spine working on a real governed path
- governed full-document retrieval over the main research path
- deterministic parse and evidence preparation strong enough to support grounded synthesis
- citations anchored to fetched evidence rather than search snippets
- one honest reviewer or operator workflow
- one truthful canonical runtime surface and run contract

It is not:

- every later optimization from the full product vision
- the whole advanced retrieval stack
- the whole self-improvement or meta-learning stack
- the whole polished consultant platform
