# Quality Gates

## Problem Frame

Passes when the governing question, decision maker, R1/R2 gap, constraints, and action implication are clear or visibly assumed.

Fails in Keystone or machine-readable mode when decision maker, R1, R2, or constraints are omitted, even if they are inferable from surrounding prose.

## Axis Fit

Passes when the selected axis exposes decision leverage and rejected axes are considered for ambiguous prompts.

## Branch Logic

Passes when every sibling group declares source logic, truth logic, and evaluation logic where relevant, and every child answers the parent question.

## MECE

Passes when branches do not materially overlap and collectively cover the universe implied by the selected axis.

## Hypothesis Discipline

Passes when provisional hypotheses include must-be-true assumptions and disconfirming tests.

## Pruning

Passes when the full tree and pruned tree are separate, with visible retain/defer/prune/merge rationale.

Fails when the pruned tree is mainly a KEEP/DEFER/PRUNE ledger or when more than 8 retained leaves survive without a second-pass pruning rationale or an explicit broad-map label.

## Leaf Actionability

Passes when every leaf has a resolution criterion, evidence requirement, expected artifact, and decision implication.

## Communication Check

Passes when the result can be summarized as one current answer plus 2-4 supporting reasons.

## Anti-Genericity

Passes when kept top-level branches include named mechanisms, measurable variables or observable proxies, decision consequences, and disconfirming tests.

## Ambiguity Escalation

Passes when material unknowns become blocking questions, explicit assumptions, or objective-definition branches.

## Eval Hygiene

Passes when examples are treated as priors, hidden rubrics stay hidden from executors, and benchmark cases are structurally distinct from skill examples.
