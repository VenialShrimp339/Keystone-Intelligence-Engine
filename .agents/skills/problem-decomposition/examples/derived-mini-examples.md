# Derived Mini Examples

These are compact examples of reasoning patterns, not templates.

## Airport Capacity

Good axis: supply versus demand. Deepen supply into runways, capacity per runway, and utilization. Deepen utilization because planners can affect operating hours, movement spacing, aircraft size, and slot policy.

Failure to avoid: a generic transport stakeholder tree that misses the bottleneck.

## Home Solar Now

Good axis: install-now hypothesis. Conditions include attractive payback, waiting not superior, material carbon reduction, and manageable tariff/subsidy risk.

Logic: partly constraint-gate, partly weighted objective if the decision maker values emissions separately from payback.

## Manufacturing Cost Reduction

Good process: screen candidate hypotheses with must-be-true assumptions, then build issue tree around the surviving hypothesis.

Example top branches: cost reduction, implementation feasibility, quality maintenance. Implementation deepens only if special facilities or skills are required.

## Hospital ED Wait Times

Good axis: operational bottleneck and causal driver. Branches should separate arrival demand, acuity mix, intake, diagnostics, treatment, staffing, bed availability, discharge, and measurement artifacts.

Failure to avoid: people/process/technology split with no throughput logic.
